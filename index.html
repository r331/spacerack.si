01 · Giant solar wings

Power straight from the source.

Huge deployable solar arrays extend from both long sides of every pod. Continuous power from the Sun — no grid interconnect, no substation queue, no land.

  • Two wings per pod, four panels each
  • Unfold in orbit, minutes after separation
  • No grid. No land. No neighbours.

02 · Half-power by design

Every pod runs at 50% power.

Less heat to reject, lower energy draw per rack, and far longer hardware life. Headroom is a feature, not waste.

50%

03 · Evaporative cooling chamber

Heat goes to deep space.

A dedicated evaporation chamber, engineered for vacuum, carries heat away from the racks and rejects it to deep space through the radiator fin array on the pod's roof.

  • Evaporation chamber coupled to every rack loop
  • Radiator fins face away from the Sun
  • No water towers, no chillers, no coolant drawn from Earth
